# BookShop - Static HTML Book Store

A complete static HTML website for an online bookstore featuring categories, contact forms, and comprehensive delivery and payment information.

## 🚀 Features

### 📚 Book Categories
- **Fiction** - Contemporary literature and classic novels
- **Non-Fiction** - Educational and informative content
- **Science Fiction** - Futuristic stories and technology
- **Mystery & Thriller** - Suspenseful and thrilling narratives
- **Romance** - Heartwarming love stories
- **Children's Books** - Educational and entertaining books for young readers
- **Biography** - Inspiring life stories
- **Educational** - Textbooks and learning materials

### 📞 Contact System
- **Contact Form** with validation
  - Name, email, phone, subject, and message fields
  - Real-time form validation
  - Multiple inquiry categories
  - Newsletter subscription option
- **Store Information**
  - Physical address and hours
  - Phone and email contacts
  - FAQ section

### 🚚 Delivery Options
- **Standard Delivery** (3-5 business days, FREE over $35)
- **Express Delivery** (1-2 business days)
- **International Shipping** (to major countries)
- **Store Pickup** (same-day, FREE)
- **Special Services** (gift wrapping, bulk orders)

### 💳 Payment Methods
- **Credit & Debit Cards** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, etc.)
- **Digital Wallets** (PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- **Bank Transfer**
- **Cash on Delivery** (selected areas)
- **Buy Now, Pay Later** (Klarna, Afterpay, etc.)
- **Gift Cards & Store Credit**
