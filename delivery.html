📜 Delivery Policies

Important Information

  • Address Accuracy: Please ensure your delivery address is correct. We cannot be responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by customers.
  • Delivery Attempts: Our carriers will attempt delivery up to 3 times. After that, packages will be returned to sender.
  • Signature Required: Orders over $100 require signature confirmation for security.
  • Weather Delays: Severe weather conditions may cause delivery delays beyond our control.
  • Holiday Shipping: Expect delays during peak holiday seasons. Order early for guaranteed holiday delivery.
  • Damaged Packages: If your package arrives damaged, please contact us immediately with photos for quick resolution.
